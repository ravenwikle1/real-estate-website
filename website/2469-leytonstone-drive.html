About this home

A move-in ready corner condo with the big-ticket updates already done.

This third-floor corner unit in Kings Pointe at Western Branch is ready for its next owner. The major systems have been updated: new HVAC (2026), air handler (2023), water heater (2026) and refrigerator (2023).

Inside, there’s fresh paint throughout, updated light fixtures, new bathroom fixtures and professionally cleaned carpets. The open kitchen, dining and living area leads out to a private balcony overlooking the lake and fountain.

The primary bedroom has its own full bath, and a second full bath serves the guest room. Laundry is in the unit, and the washer and dryer convey. You also get an assigned parking space, assigned storage, and a community clubhouse, pool and fitness room.
