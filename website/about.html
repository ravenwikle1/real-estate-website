My approach

Real estate at a human pace.

I got into this work because I like the people part of it. The house matters, obviously — but what really matters is the family standing in the driveway wondering if this is the one.

So I slow that moment down. I’ll tell you what I notice about the roof, what the comparable sales actually say, and whether the price makes sense. If a house isn’t right, I’ll say so plainly, and we’ll go look at the next one.

On the selling side, I’m hands-on: staging notes before the photographer comes, real marketing that reaches beyond the MLS feed, and weekly updates so you’re never wondering what’s happening.

And I answer my phone. That sounds like a small thing until you’ve worked with an agent who doesn’t.
