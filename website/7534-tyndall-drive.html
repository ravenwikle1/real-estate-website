About this home

One-level living in Gloucester Point, minutes from the Coleman Bridge.

A classic brick-front home on a little under half an acre, with mature landscaping and everything on one floor. Natural light runs through the living room, dining area and kitchen, and a gas fireplace anchors the main living space.

The primary suite has a soaker tub, double vanity and walk-in closet. All three bedrooms have plantation shutters. Out back, a screened porch and a deck look over a partly fenced yard with an irrigation system.

There’s a large attic ready to finish if you want more room later, an oversized paved driveway, and a generator hookup. The Coleman Bridge is minutes away, so Yorktown, Williamsburg and the Peninsula are an easy drive for work, dinner or the airport.
