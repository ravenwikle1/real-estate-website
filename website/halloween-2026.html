Halloween in Gloucester, 2026.

Trick-or-Treat on Main Street, a spooky movie night, and how to get your porch ready.

Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, and the main event in Gloucester is Trick-or-Treat on Main Street, Saturday, October 31, from 5 to 7 PM in Gloucester Courthouse. Main Street businesses hand out candy, and the road is closed to through traffic so trick-or-treaters can walk it end to end.

Below are the local Halloween events I could confirm for this week, a quick porch checklist, and a few “haunted house” sounds that usually turn out to be simple fall maintenance.

How do I get my porch ready for trick-or-treaters?

Turn on the porch light, clear the walkway and swap real candles for battery ones. Those three steps cover most of what keeps a busy porch safe. A few more details:

  • Light the way. A lit porch is the universal “come on up” sign. Solar stakes or string lights along the walk help in the dark.
  • Clear the path. Rake wet leaves off the steps, put hoses away and set pumpkins back from the edges. Long capes and costumes trip easily.
  • Go flameless. Battery tea lights look just as good in a jack-o’-lantern and won’t catch a costume or a dry cornstalk.
  • Keep pets inside. A doorbell every two minutes is a lot for a dog. A quiet back room and a treat help.
  • Heading out of town? Leave the porch light off and put an inside lamp on a timer.

Is my house haunted, or does it just need fall maintenance?

Most spooky house sounds this time of year have a very un-spooky fix. Here’s what’s usually behind them:

  • Mysterious drafts usually mean worn weatherstripping around doors and windows. It’s an easy weekend fix before the cold settles in.
  • Rattling or moaning vents often point to a clogged HVAC filter. Swap it when you switch over to heat.
  • Water where it shouldn’t be after a storm? Gutters packed with fall leaves are the usual culprit.
  • A ghostly chirp in the night is a smoke detector asking for a new battery. Daylight saving time ends Sunday, November 1, which makes a handy reminder to check them all.
