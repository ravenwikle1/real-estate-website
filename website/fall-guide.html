Local guide · Autumn 2026

Pumpkins, markets & corn maze season.

Every fall market, festival and pumpkin patch worth the drive, from Mathews to the Northern Neck.

Whether you’re settling into a new home here or you’ve been on the water your whole life, fall is one of the best times to get out and enjoy this area. Here’s what’s on the calendar — save it, share it, and pin your favorites.

Northern Neck

Lancaster · Northumberland · Westmoreland · Richmond County
Oct 4 Sat

Irvington Farmers Market & Distinctive Bazaar

9:00 AM – 1:00 PM

Irvington United Methodist Church, Irvington, VA

The monthly farmers market pairs up with the church bazaar — homemade baked goods, jams, crafts and a gift shop table, with proceeds supporting local food-bank and scholarship funds. Also happening November 1 and December 6.

Oct 10 Sat–Sun

Kilmarnock Fall Festival

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM, both days

Town Centre Park, 150 N. Main St, Kilmarnock, VA 22482

The Northern Neck’s signature autumn weekend — 50+ vendor booths, live music and dance on the main stage, a kids zone with bounce houses and a petting zoo, and a chili cook-off on Sunday from 1–2 PM.

Middle Peninsula & The Peninsula

Mathews · Gloucester · Middlesex · Williamsburg · Yorktown
Sep 24 thru Oct 31

Belmont Pumpkin Farm

Saturdays & Sundays, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM

155 Belmont Lane, North, VA 23128 (Mathews County)

Pick-your-own pumpkin patch, corn and sunflower mazes, scarecrow making, hayrides, farm animals, and a farm cafe famous for its pumpkin rolls. Weekday school tours by appointment.

Oct 10 Sat–Sun

Yorktown Market Days — Fall Festival

Weekend hours

Riverwalk Landing, 401 Water St, Yorktown, VA

40+ vendors along the river selling pumpkins, mums, kettle corn and caramel apples, plus free family fun — face painting, a hay maze and a petting zoo.

Oct 17 Sat–Sun

Yorktown Victory Celebration

Both days

American Revolution Museum at Yorktown, 200 Water St, Yorktown, VA

Marking the anniversary of the 1781 Revolutionary War victory with artillery firings, living history and hands-on activities for kids.

Oct 24 Sat

Yorktown Market Days — Paws at the River

Weekend market hours

Riverwalk Landing, Yorktown, VA

The Saturday farmers market goes pet-themed — bring the dog for costume fun alongside the usual market stalls.

Nov 6 Fri–Sat

Urbanna Oyster Festival

Both days

Downtown Urbanna, VA (Middlesex County)

Virginia’s official Oyster Festival — over 50 vendors serving oysters every way imaginable, a parade, carnival rides and crafts on the waterfront. A big regional draw, so plan for crowds and parking.

Nov 21 Sat

Yorktown Market Days — Harvest Festival

Market hours

Riverwalk Landing, Yorktown, VA

A Thanksgiving-season send-off for the market season with turkey, sweet potatoes, cornbread and pumpkin pie alongside the regular vendor lineup.

Dates, hours and admission details come from each event’s organizers and were current as of early September 2026. Farm and festival hours shift with weather and crop conditions, so double-check the event’s own page close to the date.
