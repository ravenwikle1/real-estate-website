Meet your REALTOR®

Hi, I’m Raven.

I help people find their footing in one of the prettiest corners of Virginia — the creeks, the farmland, the small downtowns, and the neighborhoods where everybody waves.

A move is rarely just a transaction. It’s a new job, a growing family, a downsize, a fresh start. My job is to make the real estate part of it feel calm and handled, so you can focus on the rest.

You’ll get straight answers, quick replies, and an agent who will tell you when a house isn’t right for you — even when it costs me the sale.
